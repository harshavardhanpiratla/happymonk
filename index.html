Questions

FAQ

What does “pure” mean at Happy Monk?

Every pack names its supplier, the certificate type, the issuing body and the certificate number — so you can check the evidence yourself.

Why only six products?

We start with the dishes people already know by taste, and only add what earns its place in that kitchen.

Where do you ship?

Happy Monk is an Indian pantry in Canada. See our shipping page for details.

What’s the difference between Cooking Ghee and Puja Ghee?

Cooking Ghee is made for the kitchen; Puja Ghee is made for the lamp. Each pack carries its own proof.
